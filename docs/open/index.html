3

Decide what it may do

Before any of the app runs, a window like this appears. The app opens with exactly what you allow -- it cannot reach the internet, or any other folder on your computer.

“Notes” wants to:

read files in its notes folderto load the notes you saved before
save files in its notes folderto keep the note you are writing
use your clipboardto copy and paste inside the app
Allow and open

It gets nothing else: no files, no internet, no camera.
