    Install Krate, once

    This is the only setup there is. Do it once and every Krate app anyone sends you from then on is just a double-click.

    On your Mac, open Terminal — press ⌘ and Space, type terminal, press Enter — then paste this and press Enter.

    curl -fsSL https://krate.tech/install.sh | sh

    Prefer not to use Terminal? Download Krate.app, unzip it, then right-click it once and choose Open.

    Open PowerShell — press Start, type powershell, press Enter — then paste this and press Enter.

    irm https://krate.tech/install.ps1 | iex

    That is the whole thing. It does not ask for an administrator password.

    Open a terminal, paste this, and press Enter.

    curl -fsSL https://krate.tech/install.sh | sh

    Nothing else to install, and no administrator password needed.

The things people ask

  • Is it safe to open?Krate shows you what the app wants and gives it nothing else. An app that asks to save a list cannot read anything else on your computer, even if it tried.
  • Do I need an account?No. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay.
  • Where does my information go?Nowhere. The app runs on your own computer, and whatever it saves stays there.
  • What if I say no?The app does not start. It cannot run halfway on less than it asked for and quietly fail later.
  • Can I change my mind later?Yes. You are asked each time it opens, so nothing you allow once is allowed forever.
