The things people ask

  • Is it safe to open?Krate shows you what the app wants and gives it nothing else. An app that asks to save a list cannot read anything else on your computer, even if it tried.
  • Do I need an account?No. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay.
  • Where does my information go?Nowhere. The app runs on your own computer, and whatever it saves stays there.
  • What if I say no?The app does not start. It cannot run halfway on less than it asked for and quietly fail later.
  • Can I change my mind later?Yes. You are asked each time it opens, so nothing you allow once is allowed forever.
