What works today

The useful parts are already real.

Krate is early, but the core product journey works now and is built in public.

One file, three systems

The same .krate app opens on Mac, Windows, and Linux.

AI authoring

krate create --agent claude lets Claude author the app from your request.

Clear requested access

The app carries the access it requests. Krate shows it before the app runs and enforces the user’s choice.

Local app behavior

Windowed apps can save locally, keep their state, and reopen later.

Planned next

Krate Cloud

People will be able to publish, find, share, and update small apps through Krate Cloud. The app still opens locally through the Krate runtime, with its requested access visible before it runs.
