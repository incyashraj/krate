Studio is where apps get made: on your computer with the AI you already use, or right here in your browser.

  1. Type it or say it. Add a picture or a file for the AI to read.

  2. Krate says what it will build and what it will ask permission for. Change it, or build it.

  3. A few minutes. The app takes shape on screen, and you can ask for changes while it works.

  4. Share the file, or a link anyone can open. Every change becomes the next version.

Studio on your computer uses the AI you already have. In the browser, your first app is on us, and your own API key keeps you going.
