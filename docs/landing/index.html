AI makes an app in minutes.
Getting it to people still takes weeks.

Shipping used to mean developer licences, a build and a signature for every OS, a different download for each, and an .exe that gets your whole PC when someone double-clicks it. Hosting a web app instead never feels native. Krate replaces all of that with one file.

A link opens it in anyone's browser, on a phone or a computer, with nothing to install and no account. With the Krate runtime installed, it runs natively, with no browser or WebView inside, and every Krate app after that is just a file.
