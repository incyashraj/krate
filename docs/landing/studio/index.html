Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe the app in plain words and read plain words back. The raw engine output is one click away if you ever want it, but nothing requires it.

Which AI does it use?

Whichever you already have. Claude, Codex, Gemini and Copilot are detected automatically, and Krate can install one for you. Your sign-in stays with that tool -- Krate never holds its keys.

Is an app I receive safe to open?

Before it runs, you see exactly what it is allowed to do -- and Krate enforces that list at the system boundary. An app cannot touch anything you did not allow, whatever its code says. If you do not have Krate yet, the first open installs it once -- about 24 MB -- and every later app is just a file.

How much does it cost?

The player -- the part that opens apps -- is free and open source, forever. Studio lets you make three apps a month free; unlimited making is $12 a month, or $96 a year. Making apps uses your own AI subscription, the one you already pay for; Krate never holds its keys.

How stable is it?

Krate Studio is the recommended way to use Krate, and it ships often: fixes and improvements land as releases, and the numbers behind them are measured and published. If something breaks or feels wrong, telling us gets it fixed fast.
